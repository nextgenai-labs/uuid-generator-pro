# Support

## Community Support

For questions, feature requests, or general discussion, please open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/nextgenai-labs/uuid-generator-pro/issues).

## Commercial Support

Customers who purchased UUID Generator Pro through Codester are eligible for support.

**Response time:** Within 48 hours (business days)

**Scope of support:**
- Installation and setup assistance
- Bug verification and reporting
- Usage guidance

**Out of scope:**
- Custom development
- Integration with third-party systems
- Code modifications beyond the original product

## Contact

Open a support ticket through your Codester purchase page, or create a GitHub issue with the label `support`.

## Before Requesting Support

1. Check [FAQ.md](./FAQ.md) for common questions.
2. Review [INSTALL.md](./INSTALL.md) for setup instructions.
3. Search existing [GitHub issues](https://github.com/nextgenai-labs/uuid-generator-pro/issues) for solutions.
